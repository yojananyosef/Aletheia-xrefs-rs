# Data sources

The code in this repository is original. The datasets bundled with it come from
third parties and keep their own licenses, which require attribution:

## Cross-references

- **OpenBible.info cross-references** — CC BY 4.0.
  <https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/>

## Bible text

- **Biblia Platense (Mons. Juan Straubinger, `spaplatense`)** — public domain
  (1948 + 70 years). Source: CrossWire Bible Society.
- **American Standard Version (ASV, `eng-asv`)** — public domain.
  Source: eBible.org.

## Original languages and lexicon

- **Reina-Valera 1909 (USFX)** — public domain. Source: eBible.org,
  <https://ebible.org/spaRV1909/>. Its Strong-tagged words are aligned to the
  TAHOT/TAGNT word order to produce the Spanish glosses and usage senses shown
  in the lexicon card.
- **STEPBible Data** (TAHOT, TAGNT, TBESH, TBESG), created by Tyndale House
  Cambridge and curated by STEPBible.org — CC BY 4.0.
  <https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data>
  - TAHOT is based on the Westminster Leningrad Codex (WLC) via OpenScriptures.
  - TAGNT amalgamates NA27/28, SBLGNT, THGNT, TR, Byz and other editions;
    SBLGNT is CC BY 4.0. The app shows the NA27/28 base and falls back to the
    Byzantine/TR text where a KJV verse has no NA witness.
  - TBESH and TBESG are brief lexicons based on abridged BDB and Abbott-Smith.

The KJV versification (chapter/verse counts) is used only as a canonical
coordinate system; its text is not bundled. The Spanish glosses and usage
senses come from the RV1909 alignment; STEPBible's full lexical articles are
shown in their original English, while the interface and morphology
descriptions are localized.


## Composition dates

The composition-date scale (shown by default) and the table on the About page
are an approximate historical aid based on the introductions and chronology of the
*Seventh-day Adventist Bible Commentary* (1980 edition),
<https://archive.org/details/SdaBibleCommentary1980>. The commentary's text is
not redistributed; only approximate year ranges are cited. The dates are not
part of the biblical text and are not presented as inspired.
